# Reader-First Comic Rebuild

## What Changed

- Six episodes received original scene-specific bitmap artwork; nine article openings, scripts and research applications were rewritten.
- The visible comic label and repetitive fictional-testimonial disclaimer were removed across the 22 articles and three homepage teasers. The fiction policy remains in each site's editorial policy.
- Research now follows the story in prose, followed by practical actions. Detailed methods and limitations remain available in an accessible disclosure. Essential medical caveats remain visible.
- A sales link no longer interrupts the research article between the comic and its explanation. Existing provider and end-of-article links remain.

## Reference Study, Not Model Training

The following legally public pages were consulted for pacing and explanatory structure. No artwork, character design, dialogue or story was copied, and no model was fine-tuned:

- Shueisha's story-building lesson and published four-panel example: https://ribonjump.shueisha.co.jp/mangakakottou/contents/island3/01/
- Shogakukan's narrative structure lesson: https://shincomi.shogakukan.co.jp/training/015.html
- KADOKAWA's publicly available beauty-science comic page and editorial description: https://www.comic-essay.com/read/218/2057.html

Plugin discovery for comic/manga storytelling returned general design tools rather than a necessary dedicated capability. No plugin was installed. Built-in image generation supplied the new art; Sharp only resized and encoded the selected images to responsive WebP.

## Art Prompt Set

Common production specification: original Japanese adult everyday educational comic; landscape 3:2; four equal quadrants, reading top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right; top 30% of each quadrant white for accessible HTML dialogue; no baked-in text, balloons, labels or logos; consistent recurring adults, expressive movement and distinct camera framing; no copying a named artist; no medical before/after or treatment testimonial.

## Reader Outcomes

The practical aim is a more concrete profile, clearer consultation questions, a usable record or a better-defined purchase decision. These are not claims that the comic or article has been proven to improve conversion, clinical outcomes or relationship success.
