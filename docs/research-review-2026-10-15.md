## Editorial Boundaries

- Comics are fictional educational conversations, not treatment testimonials.
- Practical adaptations are identified as suggestions, not tested dating interventions.
- Primary and secondary endpoints are distinguished. A nonsignificant superiority test does not establish equivalence.
- Study population, follow-up and attrition matter. Female axillary findings do not establish male beard outcomes.
- Research-stage drugs are not presented as purchasable recommendations or approved Japanese treatments. Current approval must be verified before future claims about availability.
- No prescribing doses, individualized treatment selection, attractiveness guarantees, fabricated expert review or provider rankings were added.
- Provider links and affiliate approval status remain separate from scientific citations.
- Existing illustrations were reused; nine sets of dialogue were revised, not nine new drawings generated.

Review this material before adding treatment recommendations, when a cited paper is corrected/retracted, when Japanese guidance changes, or when new trials materially change the interpretation. This file does not imply automated monitoring.
